Frequently asked questions

Everything you may want to know before we build your Eureka Select journey.

How personalised is a Eureka Select?-
We start with your dates, budget, travel style and who you're travelling with - not a ready-made package. From the route and stays to experiences and pace, we build the tour around what works for you. Have saved reels, a hotel you love or a restaurant you absolutely want to try? Send them our way.
What do we take care of?+
Almost everything. We can arrange stays, transfers, experiences, sightseeing and local guides, along with pre-departure and on-ground support. What's included will always be clearly mentioned in your final proposal, so you know exactly what's sorted before you go.
Flights, visa & travel insurance?+
Yes. We can help you find and book the right flights, or you're welcome to book them yourself. We'll also guide you through the visa, entry requirements and assist with the visa process where needed.
Can you plan honeymoons, families and bigger groups?+
Absolutely. We plan tours for couples, honeymoons, families, friends, celebrations and private groups - because a honeymoon for two and a holiday for twenty definitely shouldn't look the same. Travelling with a bigger group? We can arrange group-friendly stays, transport and experiences, with special group rates where available.

Can you arrange something special during the tour?+
Absolutely. Birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, private dinners, surprises or that place you've had saved on Instagram for six months - tell us what you have in mind. If we can make it happen, we'll find a way to work it in.

Can I pay in instalments or EMI?+
Yes. You don't always have to pay for the entire tour at once. Flexible payment options and EMI may be available, depending on your tour and booking value. We'll share the available options before you confirm.
What happens once I confirm my tour?+
Once you're happy with the itinerary, we'll take care of the bookings, confirmations and coordination. You'll receive your travel details and vouchers before departure, while we keep track of everything behind the scenes. You pack. We'll handle the rest.
What if I need help while I'm travelling?+
You won't be left figuring things out alone. Our team and on-ground partners stay connected throughout your tour to help with your arrangements and step in if something needs attention.
