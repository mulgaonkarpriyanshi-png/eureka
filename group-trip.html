DAY 1▾

Arrive in Manila

Touchdown at Manila Airport and without wasting a second – head straight to your hotel in the heart of the traveller's district. Meet up with your travel crew and get excited for the adventure to come.

Canyoneering in Moalboal

Head to Moalboal for a full day of canyoneering — natural rock slides, cliff jumps, and swims through jungle pools, followed by a snorkel alongside the famous sardine run.
