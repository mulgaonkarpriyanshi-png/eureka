Privacy Policy

At Eureka Trips, your privacy is important to us, and we take care to protect your data. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you participate in our group trips or book custom travel experiences. We've made it simple and transparent for you. But if you're unsure about anything just contact us.

Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Information We Collect

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you join our group trips or plan a customized travel experience, we may collect personal details, including name, email address, phone number, and payment information.

Travel Preferences

We'd like to gather information about your travel interests to tailor our trips to your needs. This may include preferred destinations, activities, special interests, group information (if booking for multiple travellers)

Non-Personal Information

We collect non-personal data through cookies and other tracking technologies, such as browser type, IP address, and general location.

How We Use Your Information

How We Use Your Information

To Foster a Sense of Community

For group trips, we use your data to connect you with other travellers in your age group, fostering shared experiences and camaraderie.

To Coordinate Group and Private Trips

Your information helps us organize trips that cater to your preferences, ensuring activities, accommodations, and itineraries are suited to your travel goals.

To Create Tailored Experiences

For customized trips, your data allows us to design personalized itineraries, accommodations, and activities that meet your specific interests, whether it's a family vacation, a honeymoon, or a solo adventure.

Marketing Communications

With your consent, we may send you updates about upcoming trips, special promotions, and travel tips that match your interests.

Legal Compliance

We may use your information to comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Legal Compliance

Fellow Travelers

For group trips, we may share relevant trip details with other group members to ensure a smooth and seamless experience.

Third-Party Service Providers

We collaborate with trusted partners to provide accommodations, activities, and other services for both group and customized trips.

Legal Requirements

If required by law, or to protect the rights of Eureka Trips, we may disclose your information to legal authorities.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and personalize content. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Data Security

Eureka Trips employs advanced security measures, including encryption and secure servers, to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Protection Rights

Access and Correction

You can request access to the personal information we hold about you and ask for corrections if necessary.

Opt-Out

You may opt out of receiving marketing communications from us at any time.

Data Deletion

You can request the deletion of your data, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
