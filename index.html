Why Travel With Eureka Trips

The little things that make every Eureka trip different.

Inclusions, actually included
Unlike most other tour companies, we don't do hidden surprises. What we promise is what you get. Most of the big experiences are already included in your trip cost, with a few optional ones, so you can just enjoy the journey and feel good knowing exactly where your money is going.
Awesome crew & support
From the moment you plan till the trip ends, you are never on your own. Our team, trip leaders, and local insiders are there to guide, support, and make things smooth, so your only job is to travel and have the best time of your life.
Unique experiences
We do more than tick off tourist spots. Every Eureka trip is built with social experiences, local moments, and memorable moments that make the destination feel deeper, richer, and way more exciting than a regular holiday.
Better accommodation
We handpick stays that feel special, comfortable, and worth it. From stylish city hotels to charming stays in nature, you get a place that adds to the experience, not just a bed to sleep in. All included and booked in advance for you.
Travel solo without oh-no
We believe travel comes with a responsibility. On all our trips you'll live life through a local lens and make a real difference through our Small Change initiative. We are proud to say each of our tours are 100% carbon neutral!
Book with confidence
We keep things simple, safe, and stress-free. With flexible changes, easy payments, and a trusted booking process, you can plan your trip with confidence and focus on the fun part instead of the admin.
