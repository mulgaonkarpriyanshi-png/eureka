How does it work?−
  • Read through this page so you know exactly what's included and what's not.
  • Got questions? Reach our team over call, WhatsApp, or email — they know these trips inside out.
  • Hit the Book button and fill out a short form.
  • We'll send payment details by email for you to complete.
  • Once you're confirmed, we'll send your booking confirmation and help with flights and visas.
You'll also be added to the trip WhatsApp group 1–2 weeks before departure.
Who can join this trip?+
Anyone aged 21–39 can join. You're welcome to come solo, with a friend or partner, or as part of a group — either way, you'll be travelling alongside young travellers just like you.
What documents will I need?+
  • A passport valid for at least 6 months
  • Visa-on-arrival is free for Indians for stays up to 14 days (destination dependent)
  • Confirmed hotel booking voucher
  • Return flight tickets
  • Proof of sufficient funds
What about flights?+
Book your arrival flight to land before 4 PM on Day 1. Your return flight can be booked from either the trip's start or end city, whichever suits you better — our team will help you plan whichever works best, including any extension you'd like to add on. Airport pickup isn't included, but shared airport shuttles are easy to arrange, and our team helps coordinate them with fellow travellers landing around the same time.
What's not included that I should budget for?+
Your trip cost already covers daily breakfast, a couple of lunches, one local dinner, activities, sightseeing and plenty more. The main extra costs to plan for are your international flights, any remaining meals, and local airport transfers — visa-on-arrival is typically free for Indian travellers. We're happy to help you budget based on your own travel style.
Are there optional activities?+
Yes — depending on the destination, things like surfing lessons, spa treatments, cooking classes, ATV rides, scuba upgrades, and day trips to nearby islands are available as add-ons. These can be booked on the ground with your trip leader, or in advance with our help.
Can I extend my trip?+
Absolutely — add a few extra days before or after your trip and our team will help sort out hotels and activities tailored to what you want to do with the extra time.
What does the Eureka Trips team actually do?+
We handle the logistics so you don't have to — answering questions, helping with flights or extra nights, and sending you a full trip dossier beforehand with everything you need to know. On the trip itself, your dedicated trip leader is there the whole way, planning the days, finding the best food, and keeping the group's energy up.
What's the group like?+
Our groups typically run around 60% women and 40% men, and most travellers join solo — the rest come along with friends, partners, or siblings. It's a genuinely mixed, easygoing crowd.
