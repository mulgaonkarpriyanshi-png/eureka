Philippines Hop & Chill

Manila➾ Cebu➾ Puerto Princesa➾ El Nido
Itinerary Reviews FAQ's Book Now

Mezcal in Mexico City, Chichén Itzá at sunrise and a week on the Caribbean coast. 14 days through Mexico.

Blissful white-sand beaches, palm-lined shores, and crystal-blue waters, the Philippines' absolute paradise. Picture yourself canyoning through jungle swimming holes, tackling natural rock slides and cliff jumps, snorkeling the rare sardine run, and chasing unforgettable sunset hikes. In Palawan, arguably the best spot in the Philippines (some say the world!) Hop into a colourful Filipino jeepney, chase hidden waterfalls, explore magical underground rivers, and sail off on dreamy island-hopping trips.

Who can join this trip? +

This trip is designed for solo travellers and small groups aged 21-39 who are comfortable with an active, social itinerary. No prior travel experience is needed — just an open mind and a love for adventure.

What's the group size on this trip? +

Our groups typically range from 10 to 16 travellers, kept small enough to feel personal while still giving you a real crew to explore with.

Do I need travel insurance? +

Yes, travel insurance is mandatory for all our trips and is not included in the trip price. We recommend arranging cover as soon as you book.

Can I book a private room? +

Absolutely. Rooms are twin-share by default, but private room upgrades are available for an additional cost, subject to availability.
