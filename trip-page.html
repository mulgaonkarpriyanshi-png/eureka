Philippines Hop & Chill

Manila➾ Cebu➾ Puerto Princesa➾ El Nido

Mezcal in Mexico City, Chichén Itzá at sunrise and a week on the Caribbean coast. 14 days through Mexico.

Blissful white-sand beaches, palm-lined shores, and crystal-blue waters, the Philippines' absolute paradise. Picture yourself canyoning through jungle swimming holes, tackling natural rock slides and cliff jumps, snorkeling the rare sardine run, and chasing unforgettable sunset hikes. In Palawan, arguably the best spot in the Philippines (some say the world!) Hop into a colourful Filipino jeepney, chase hidden waterfalls, explore magical underground rivers, and sail off on dreamy island-hopping trips.

Trip Logistics:

  • 7 nights of accommodations in Lecce (2 nights), Matera (1 night), Sorrento (3 nights), and Rome (1 night) (shared twin room with one other traveler, optional private upgrade subject to availability)
  • 7 breakfasts, 3 lunches, and 4 dinners throughout the trip, including curated welcome and farewell dinners
  • Private transportation between cities and to all included activities

Included Experiences:

  • Welcome drinks and orientation to kick off the trip and meet your travel crew
  • Guided walking tour of Matera's ancient Sassi districts with a local expert, exploring one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited settlements
  • Morning visit to Matera's historic thermal baths for a rejuvenating soak inside the city's cave-carved spa complex
  • Guided visit to the coastal town of Polignano a Mare, famous for its cliffside views
