What does Eureka mean?−
"Eureka" is that moment when you feel like shouting, "I found it!" - when something suddenly clicks. We think travel is full of them: discovering a place you didn't expect to love, seeing something differently or meeting someone who changes the trip completely. We call those Eureka moments.
What exactly do we do?+
Eureka creates social group trips for like-minded travellers. We sort the stays, transport and experiences, bring together a great crew and have an awesome Trip Leader along for the ride. You just show up ready to explore.

Want something more personal? We also create custom journeys through Eureka Select.
Who joins our group trips?+
Curious travellers in their 20s and 30s who want more than the usual group trip. Different cities, careers and backgrounds. Some come for the adventure, some for the culture, some for the people - most want a bit of everything.

You don't need to be an experienced traveller. You just need to be up for it.
Can I join a Eureka trip solo?+
Absolutely. Around 70% of our travellers join solo, so showing up without knowing anyone is completely normal here. Your Trip Leader knows exactly what they've signed up for - breaking the ice is part of the job.
Can I join with a friend or partner?+
Of course. Bring your best friend, sibling or partner. We usually keep friend groups to 3-4 people max, so the trip stays social and everyone mixes in.
Why only ages 21-39?+
Because who you travel with can change the whole trip. Keeping everyone within a similar age group helps us get the pace, experiences, free time and nights out right. If you've travelled with us before, you probably already get it.
I'm over 39. Can I still travel with Eureka?+
Absolutely. The 21-39 age limit only applies to our social group trips. With Eureka Select, we create custom journeys around your dates, destination, travel style and who you're travelling with - with no group-trip age limit.
What is Eureka Select?+
Eureka Select is our personalised side of travel. It's for couples, honeymoons, families, friends and private groups who want something made for them. No fixed dates. No fixed itineraries. Just your trip, your way.
Where does Eureka travel?+
Across India and around the world. From island hopping in the Philippines and Full Moon nights in Thailand to Nile cruises in Egypt, cafe-hopping through Europe and chasing the Northern Lights in the Arctic - we've been there, planned it and probably have a story from it. Take your pick, or let us inspire you.
